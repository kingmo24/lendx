⚖️ Important Disclosure & Consent — Required Before Proceeding

Please read and agree to the following disclosures as required under Canadian federal and provincial law.

1. Identity & Regulatory Status

LendX Financial Inc. ("LendX") is a financial services company operating in Canada. This platform offers short-term loan products and related financial services. LendX operates subject to applicable provincial licensing requirements. Licensing status varies by province — check lendxfinancial.com/licences for current status in your province.

2. Cost of Borrowing Disclosure (Federal & Provincial)

As required by the Criminal Code of Canada (s. 347) and applicable provincial payday lending legislation, the following cost of borrowing applies:

  • Ontario: $14 per $100 borrowed — Payday Loans Act, 2008
  • British Columbia / Alberta: $15 per $100 — BC Business Practices & Consumer Protection Act; Alberta Consumer Protection Act
  • Manitoba / Saskatchewan: $17 per $100
  • Nova Scotia: $19 per $100
  • New Brunswick / PEI: $15–$25 per $100 (varies)

Example (Ontario): A $500 loan for 14 days = $70 borrowing fee, $570 total repayment. Annualized Percentage Rate (APR) is approximately 365%. Short-term loans are not intended as a long-term financial solution.

Loans are not available in all provinces and territories. Quebec residents are not eligible for this product.

3. Privacy & Consent to Collect Personal Information (PIPEDA / Law 25)

By proceeding, you expressly consent to LendX Financial Inc. collecting, using, and disclosing your personal information for the following purposes, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation:

  • Processing your loan or credit application
  • Verifying your identity and creditworthiness (soft inquiry — no impact to your credit score)
  • Communicating with you about your application and account
  • Complying with legal, regulatory, and anti-money laundering obligations
  • Fraud prevention and security purposes

Your information will not be sold to third parties for marketing purposes. You may withdraw consent or request access/correction of your personal information at any time by contacting privacy@lendxfinancial.com. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

4. Credit Inquiry Consent

By submitting a loan application, you authorize LendX Financial Inc. and its designated credit bureau partners (Equifax Canada Co. and/or TransUnion of Canada Inc.) to conduct a soft credit inquiry solely for the purpose of assessing your application. A soft inquiry does not affect your credit score and will not be visible to other lenders. If you proceed to a full loan agreement, a hard inquiry may be conducted — you will be separately notified and asked to consent before any hard pull occurs.

5. No Guarantee of Approval

Submission of this form does not constitute a loan agreement, guarantee of approval, or commitment to lend. All applications are subject to eligibility assessment, identity verification, and creditworthiness review. LendX reserves the right to decline any application at its sole discretion, consistent with applicable human rights and anti-discrimination legislation.

6. Electronic Communications Consent (CASL)

You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting support@lendxfinancial.com.

7. Risk Disclosure

Short-term borrowing products carry significant financial risk, including the potential for a cycle of debt if not repaid on time. LendX strongly encourages you to:

  • Borrow only what you can afford to repay
  • Consider all available options, including credit unions, bank overdraft facilities, and government assistance programs
  • Review your provincial consumer protection resources before proceeding
