Calculator FAQ
Answers to common questions about loan costs and calculations.
How does early repayment save money?
Interest accrues on your outstanding balance each month. The sooner you reduce that balance to zero, the fewer months of interest you pay. Even paying a few months early on a medium-term loan can save hundreds of dollars.
Does LendX charge prepayment penalties?
No — LendX charges zero penalties for early or extra payments on all loan products. Many traditional lenders charge 1–3% of the outstanding balance, so always check before repaying early.
How are extra payments applied?
Extra payments are applied directly to your principal balance, not future interest. This accelerates payoff and reduces the total interest you pay. LendX allows unlimited extra payments at no charge.
Why is APR so high for short-term loans?
APR is annualized. A $14/$100 fee for 14 days extrapolates to ~365% APR — but the actual dollar cost is just $112 on an $800 loan. APR is a useful comparison tool but must be interpreted in context of the loan term.
What's the difference between APR and interest rate?
The interest rate is the base cost of borrowing. APR (Annual Percentage Rate) includes all fees — origination fees, administration fees — giving a fuller picture of total cost. Always compare APR when shopping loans.
Are these calculators accurate for my LendX loan?
These tools use standard amortization and payday loan formulas. Your actual loan agreement will govern exact amounts. Apply to get a precise, personalized offer with guaranteed rates — no credit impact to check.
