4. Borrower Rights

As a borrower of a short-term (payday) loan from LendX Financial, you have the following rights under applicable Canadian law:

Right to Full Disclosure

You have the right to receive, before signing any loan agreement, a written disclosure of: the total cost of borrowing in dollars, the annual percentage rate (APR), all fees, the due date, and the full terms of repayment.

Right to Cancel

In Ontario, you have the right to cancel a payday loan agreement without penalty by the end of the next business day after signing. To cancel: return the borrowed funds in full to LendX's office or designated account within that time.

Right Against Rollovers

You cannot be required or induced to roll over your payday loan. LendX is prohibited by law from extending, renewing, or replacing a payday loan with another payday loan if the result would be continuous indebtedness exceeding 62 days.

Right to Extended Repayment

If you have already borrowed and repaid at least two payday loans in the preceding 63-day period, you have the right to request an extended payment plan at no additional cost. Contact LendX before the due date to arrange this.

Right Against Harassment

LendX and its collection agents (including NEO Legal Services PC) are prohibited from using harassing, threatening, or abusive conduct in any collection activity. All collections comply with Ontario's Collection and Debt Settlement Services Act.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

You may file a complaint with:

  • Ontario: Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery (Consumer Protection Ontario)
  • FCAC: Financial Consumer Agency of Canada — canada.ca/fcac

6. FINTRAC / AML Compliance

LendX Financial Inc. is subject to Canada's Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (PCMLTFA) and is registered with or in the process of registering with the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC) as a money services business.

Our AML/ATF compliance program includes:

  • Client Identification: All borrowers are required to verify their identity before receiving funds, using government-issued photo ID and other documentary methods as required by FINTRAC guidelines.
  • Beneficial Ownership: For business applicants, we identify all individuals who own or control 25% or more of the entity.
  • PEP Screening: We screen all clients against Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) and Head of International Organizations (HIO) lists.
  • Transaction Monitoring: We monitor transactions for indicators of money laundering, fraud, or terrorist financing.
  • Record Keeping: We retain all verification records for a minimum of 5 years as required by FINTRAC regulations.
  • Suspicious Transaction Reporting: We report suspicious transactions and other reportable events to FINTRAC as required by law.

Our compliance officer can be reached at hello@lendxfinancial.com.
